Put the MySQL JDBC driver in this folder before building.

1. Go to https://dev.mysql.com/downloads/connector/j/
2. Choose "Platform Independent" and download the ZIP archive.
3. Unzip it and copy mysql-connector-j-9.x.x.jar into this lib folder.

build.bat / run.bat (Windows) and build.sh / run.sh (Mac/Linux) pick up every jar in lib automatically.
